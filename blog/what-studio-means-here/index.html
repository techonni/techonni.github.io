What “studio” means here

Not an agency. Not a signal room. A small desk that ships files and one clear classroom.

Techonni is a studio because the work is made here and sold here. There is no client roster on the home. There is a shop of prints and a desk called Euro Desk FX. That is the list.

I use the word studio the way a print shop might: a room with a few tools, a name on the door, and a habit of finishing things. The hub at www.techonni.com is that door. shop.techonni.com is the rack of posters. fx.techonni.com is the classroom off to the side.

Design

The first product is visual. Seventeen Bauhaus posters as print-ready JPEGs. Same size. Same price. A bundle if you want the language, not one sheet. The site that sells them uses the same grey, the same type, the same mark as the hub. If the checkout looks like another company, the studio broke.

More objects can sit on that rack later. The rule stays: a file you can use without a tutorial. A price you can read without a slash. No countdown.

One education desk

Euro Desk FX is not the whole brand. It is one product: beginner English on EURUSD and GBPUSD. Sessions, spreads, journals, a line that signals are not advice. The guides live on the fx site. They do not flood the hub. If you came for a print, you should not have to step over a candlestick.

The Telegram channel and the fx blog are for people who asked for that desk. The hub only points. Subscribe on the hub if you want studio notes — prints, process, the next object. Subscribe on fx if you want the pairs.

What I will not pretend

This is not a venture. It is not a course funnel with seven upsells. It is not financial advice dressed as a poster shop. If a product cannot be explained in two lines under Creating, it does not belong on the home.

Studio, here, means I will keep the list short and the files large. You can ignore the classroom. You can ignore the prints. The door stays the same either way.
